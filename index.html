How we work

No retainer before there is a reason for one.

Every engagement starts with something concrete and free. If we cannot find you a reason to keep talking, that is a useful answer too — and it costs you an hour, not a quarter.

  1. A real conversation

    Thirty minutes on what you are actually trying to do this year and what is in the way. No pitch deck, no discovery framework.

  2. Something concrete, free

    Usually a statement review or a capital pre-qualification. Either way you leave with numbers you did not have, whether or not you work with us.

  3. A scoped recommendation

    What we would do, in what order, and what it costs. Where the right answer is "nothing yet," we say so.

  4. Execution, with you

    We stay on the implementation. Processing placement, funding, or growth work — whichever arms the plan needs.

Where most people start

Two front doors

Free · No obligation

The statement review

Send us a recent processing statement. We return a line-by-line breakdown of every fee on it, what each one is for, which are negotiable, and what the same volume would cost elsewhere.

  • Effective rate calculated properly, not the headline number
  • Interchange, assessments, and markup separated out
  • Junk fees and padded add-ons identified by name
  • A written comparison you can take to your current provider
Request a review

Free · Soft pre-qualification

Capital pre-qualification

Tell us your monthly volume and time in business. We will tell you what range of capital is realistically available to you, what it would cost in total dollars, and whether we think it is worth taking.

  • Indicative range before any hard credit pull
  • Total cost of capital in dollars, not just a factor rate
  • Multiple funding partners compared
  • A straight answer when the timing is wrong

Who you are dealing with

A small firm, on purpose.

Atlas Dominion is deliberately small. Whoever takes your first call is who does the work and stays on the account.

That is a constraint we chose. It caps how many clients we can carry, and it means we turn down work that is not a fit rather than staffing it out to someone junior. In exchange you get an advisor who knows your numbers from memory and picks up when you call.
