About

Small on purpose.

Atlas Dominion Holdings is an owner-operated advisory firm with two operating arms behind it. The person who takes your first call is the person who does the work.

The idea

Advice that has to survive contact with the invoice.

There is a version of consulting where the engagement ends at the recommendation. The deck lands, everyone agrees it is sharp, and nothing changes — because the recommendation assumed a pricing structure the business does not have, or capital it cannot access, or margin that disappears once you account for what it costs to actually get paid.

Atlas Dominion is built the other way round. We kept the operating pieces — merchant services and business capital — precisely because they are where good plans usually die. When we tell you to reprice, we can show you what your processing actually costs. When we tell you to expand, we can tell you what funding is genuinely available and what it would cost you in dollars.

That is the whole thesis: keep the advice and the constraints in the same room.

How we operate

Four commitments

We tell you when the answer is no

Declining to place a deal costs us a commission. Placing a bad one costs you a great deal more. We would rather lose the transaction than be the reason a business took capital it could not service.

You always know how we are paid

Advisory is a direct fee. Merchant services and capital pay us through the processor or funder. Before any work starts you will be told which applies, because you cannot judge advice without knowing the incentive behind it.

Numbers you can verify yourself

Every figure in a statement audit can be checked against your own statement. We write the comparison down and hand it to you, including when clients use it as leverage with their existing provider.

No handoffs

We stay capped at a client count one person can actually hold. It means we say no to work, and it means nobody inherits your account from someone who has moved on.

Leadership

Christian

Founder, Atlas Dominion Holdings LLC

Atlas Dominion Holdings is led by its founder, who works directly on every engagement. The firm was built around a simple observation: the businesses that most need good advice are the ones least served by traditional consulting, and the parts of their operation that quietly decide profitability — what they pay to accept money, and what capital costs them — are the parts nobody wants to explain.

If you want to know whether there is a fit, the fastest route is a conversation rather than a page about us.
