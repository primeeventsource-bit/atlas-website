Engagement

How it is structured

Advisory is billed directly on a scoped fee agreed before work starts — never a percentage of anything, and never contingent on you taking a processing or capital placement. Those arms exist to serve the plan; they are not the price of admission to it.

Most engagements begin with a short diagnostic: a working session, a look at the numbers, and a written recommendation of what to do first. From there it is either a defined project or ongoing advisory, depending on whether you need a decision or a partner.

We turn work down. If what you need is a specialist we are not, or the timing is wrong, we would rather say it in the first conversation than bill you to discover it in month three.
